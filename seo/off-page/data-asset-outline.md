### Proposed Survey Questions

> Grouped by topic. Use Google Forms or Typeform for distribution via WhatsApp/email.

#### A. Business Profile (5 questions)
1. What type of business do you operate? (Multi-select: Hotel, Restaurant, Gym, School, Real Estate, Corporate Office, Retail, Spa/Wellness, Photography Agency, Other)
2. In which city/state is your primary business located? (Open text)
3. How many locations/branches do you operate? (1, 2–5, 6–10, 11+)
4. What is your approximate monthly marketing budget? (Under ₹10K, ₹10K–50K, ₹50K–1L, ₹1L–5L, ₹5L+, Prefer not to say)
5. How do most of your customers discover your business? (Multi-select: Google Search, Google Maps, Instagram, WhatsApp referral, Walk-in, Other)

#### B. Virtual Tour Adoption (6 questions)
6. When did you first publish a 360° virtual tour on Google Maps? (Year/month selector)
7. How many 360° panoramic scenes are currently live on your Google listing? (1–5, 6–15, 16–30, 30+)
8. Before using a virtual tour, what was your primary method of showcasing your space online? (Static photos, Video, Floor plan, None)
9. Who captured your 360° photos? (Professional photographer, In-house staff, PanoPublish team, Other)
10. What 360° camera do you use? (Ricoh Theta, Insta360, GoPro Max, DSLR pano head, Don't know/hired photographer, Other)
11. Did you evaluate other virtual tour platforms before choosing PanoPublish? (Yes — which ones? / No)

#### C. Business Impact (7 questions)
12. After publishing your virtual tour, did you notice a change in customer enquiries via Google? (Significant increase, Slight increase, No change, Decrease, Too early to tell)
13. Approximately what percentage increase in Google Maps profile views did you observe? (Open number or range)
14. Did the virtual tour reduce the number of "can I see the space?" phone calls or visits? (Yes — by how much approx? / No change)
15. Has the virtual tour directly contributed to a booking, sale, or sign-up that you can identify? (Yes — please describe / No / Unsure)
16. On a scale of 1–10, how important is the Google Maps virtual tour to your overall marketing? (1 = not important, 10 = critical)
17. Would you recommend 360° virtual tours to other businesses in your industry? (Definitely, Probably, Neutral, Probably not, Definitely not)
18. What is the single biggest benefit you've experienced from having a virtual tour? (Open text)

#### D. Pricing & Platform Experience (4 questions)
19. How important is INR pricing (vs USD) when choosing a software tool? (Very important, Somewhat, Not important)
20. Which payment method did you use to subscribe? (UPI, Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking, EMI)
21. Have you used the WhatsApp support channel? (Yes — satisfied? / No)
22. What feature would you most like PanoPublish to add next? (Open text)

#### E. Industry Outlook (3 questions)
23. Do you plan to shoot additional virtual tours in the next 12 months? (Yes, Maybe, No)
24. Are you considering using virtual tours for additional locations or purposes? (Yes — describe / No)
25. Do you believe virtual tours will become a standard expectation for businesses in your industry within 2 years? (Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly disagree)
