## Contact Discovery Strategy

For entries marked `[FIND VIA LinkedIn/Twitter]`:

1. **LinkedIn:** Search "[Publication Name] + journalist/editor + real estate/proptech/technology"
2. **X (Twitter):** Search bios mentioning the publication name
3. **Muck Rack / Skribe:** Media database tools that filter by beat and publication
4. **Publication "Contact Us" page:** Often has editorial email or submission guidelines
5. **Recent article bylines:** Click author name → author bio often has contact info or social links
